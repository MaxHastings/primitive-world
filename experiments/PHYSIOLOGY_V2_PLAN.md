# Physiology-v2 development contract — not a release or final validation

This branch is an explicit alternative body/controller model. Main remains
recurrent-v1. The broad acceptance goal in GOAL_CONTRACT.md is unchanged.
No evidence currently demonstrates that this new model supports better evolved
adaptation. The initial development campaign below cannot establish that claim.

## Model choices and their justification

- Eight fixed-compass point food probes at distances4 and24, plus underfoot food.
  Remove the attention actuator and its input. This resolves the conflict between
  compass-based authored food-following weights and rotating sensor slots by
  choosing a simpler sensing body. It is not the only possible resolution and
  removes an available capability, not merely a faulty line of arithmetic.
- Gathering remains an action, with underfoot availability, competition,
  inventory capacity and chosen amount. Digestion becomes physiology: after
  collection, before movement/metabolism, consume at most0.1 carried food/tick,
  limited by inventory and the energy cap100; conversion8 energy/food. There is
  no automatic gathering, new food, hunger-triggered departure or destination.
  This prevents an empty-inventory eating action from occupying the controller.
  It also changes energy timing and frees the action channel. Better survival
  across the model boundary would therefore not prove better intelligence.
- Keep recurrent tanh state16, mutation, max speed1.2, motor gain4, metabolic
  cost0.06, movement cost0.01, food regeneration0.01, reproduction costs,
  finite ecology and resource renewal schedule. No new travel reward or memory.
- Six action choices: none, collect, transfer, force, emit, reproduce. Movement
  remains simultaneous and voluntary, with continuous effort and no minimum.
  Four neighbor target logits, amount and signal payload remain controller-owned.
- Input63/output14/gene1518 layout is new, model physiology-v2, bank version3,
  checkpoint14. Never reinterpret v1 genes/checkpoints. Preserve old artifacts.

## Authored starting policy and selection

The frozen default128-genome bank is UNPREPARED. Its template relays energy,
inventory, underfoot food, near compass food and age into selected hidden units.
Action biases favor gathering where food is present and reproduction at high
reserves; opposed near-food samples influence motor outputs. These are explicit
authored starting dispositions, not learned competence or biological necessities.
All genes receive deterministic standing noise in[-0.01,0.01], generated by the
LCG in src/founders.rs from seed0x184a2321. No v1 prepared bank is transplanted.
Ordinary offspring copy genes with independent2% mutation in[-0.03,0.03], clipped
to[-4,4], and start with empty hidden state. Weights do not learn within a life.

Preparation is not yet specified or demonstrated. Existing export samples up to
128 living descendant bodies in deterministic lineage-hash order. Family size
therefore affects representation. This is an authored sampling rule, not a loss
function or a guaranteed selection for migration. No export is used in the
development campaign below. A future prepared comparison must use the same v2
body, initialization procedure, budgets and ordinary reproduction as its baseline.

## Registered initial development campaign

After passing physics/layout/controller/persistence tests, freeze the executable,
this plan and runner hashes and the actual embedded unprepared bank. Extract the
bank from a zero-tick report (no selection); then load that same frozen bank in
every world. Run sequentially: seed808 repeat1, seed909, seed1001, seed808 repeat2.
These seeds are already consumed development data, never final holdout seeds.
Each world runs200,000 ticks or extinction,1000 initial bodies, default parameters
above, evolving landscape and ordinary force/signals enabled. Metrics every1024
ticks, sampled journey evidence every32. No founder export, famine intervention,
rescue, parameter adjustment or favorable rerun. The second808 is a registered
replicate, not a replacement. Preserve every outcome and numerical failure.

Record lifespan/population/births/death accounting, invalids, cap exposure and all
journey stages. Use the existing schema2 event definitions without threshold
changes. It does not yet attribute journeys to individual major renewal cycles;
it is not the final migration acceptance instrument. Last-tick feedback sampled
every32 ticks can miss feeding and whole short journeys. Global nearest-food
queries are observer-only and do not select founders or inform controllers.

Interpretation: establish whether this internally consistent alternative exhibits
obvious physical failures, saturation, extinction or sampled journeys. Compare
808 replicates to expose variability. Do not attribute a difference from v1 to
any one change: sensors, digestion, action layout and starting bank all differ.
Do not call a long world a successful pretraining run or promote this branch on
survival alone. The next decision must consider evidence and the model contract,
not change the acceptance target to match the observed result.
