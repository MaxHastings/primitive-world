# Next phase: closed life cycle and whole-system audit

This is the handoff requested after the preservation/climate branch. It records
future work, not new biological rules or claims that the audit is complete.

## Authority and scope

The user's original kickoff is the governing intent: examine the whole project
for coherent, general mechanisms that permit emergent ecology, behavior, memory,
communication, care and evolution; identify shortcuts, missing opportunities and
cross-system problems while preserving immersion and existing capabilities.
Later assistant-written recommendations are hypotheses to assess critically,
not an approved architecture, mandatory checklist or predetermined solution.
The user explicitly clarified this after reviewing this handoff.

The closed-life-cycle test is one important investigation, not the whole audit.
Do not assume proposals such as reservoir gating, clonal/multi-source reproduction,
unconditioned climate starts, food release or a particular bootstrap success rate
are requirements. Validate their premises against the actual implementation and
the user's intent, consider alternatives, and retain useful existing mechanisms
when the evidence favors them. Speculative examples in the kickoff likewise
describe possibilities rather than behaviors the simulation must produce.

## Governing intent

Preserve the old game's presentation, controls, scale and rich interaction space.
Minimize built-in behavioral meanings while retaining composable mechanisms.
Audit ecology, physiology, perception, actions, cognition, reproduction/heredity,
development/social opportunity, and evolution together. A local improvement must
not silently remove capabilities in another subsystem.

For each subsystem document authored laws, agent-visible measurements, actions,
hereditary variables, observer-only variables, engineering limits and shortcuts.
Check physical reachability, unintended bypasses and persistence/selection leaks.
Signals need no prescribed meaning or required role in successful continuation.

## Evidence before changes

1. Strengthen the controlled test into a repeatedly self-funding loop: gather,
   digest, move, produce packets, fuse, provision juveniles, mature, independently
   gather, reproduce again. Use real costs and a declared simple controller.
   The current six-adult finite-reserve test reaches depth-two birth, but is not
   this stronger test. It has generous initialization and externally controlled
   action readouts; it does not prove a heritable policy is discoverable.
2. Predeclare a larger unselected seed battery, horizon and acceptable bootstrap
   rate before running it. Current evidence is 0/10, not 0/1,000. Measure failure
   stages separately; do not replace the original criterion after seeing results.
3. Track individual offspring through transfers, maturation and later successful
   reproduction. Family aggregates alone cannot link those events to one body.
   Preserve incarnation identity, event timing, received quantities and energy
   trajectories. Signaling is optional, not a pass condition. Keep a life-cycle
   stage metric separate from existing genealogical ancestry depth.
4. Retain the separate transfer-dependency control. Once a predeclared reachable
   bootstrap rate is established, freeze biology and observe long-run evolution
   instead of tuning toward recognizable care, intelligence or communication.

See [current evidence](juvenile-validation.md) and [preservation contract](recovery.md).

## Specific architectural questions

- Audit the cross-world hereditary reservoir: does recording a genome at birth
  preserve lineages whose offspring never develop? Trace actual writes and
  sampling before proposing an alternative. Maturation or reproduction gates
  also introduce selection assumptions and need explicit assessment.
- The current build conditions new worlds on a wet, mild initial climate, as
  explicitly requested. The later notes propose unconditioned climate sampling
  and pre-agent ecological spin-up. Compare those as separately declared
  experimental protocols; do not silently reinterpret the shipped cohort.
- Current reproduction retains obligate different-producer packet fusion. The
  archived material rewrite's single-/multi-source construction is not active.
  Audit the tradeoffs before any reintegration; preserve old capabilities.
- Nutrient/water ecology is an open local subsystem, not closed material
  conservation or lateral hydrology. Identify simplifications before adding
  mechanisms for fragmentation, refuges and reconnection.
- Retain recurrent gating, per-unit plasticity, variable expressed capacity,
  inherited mutation controls and anonymous sensing. Do not presume heritable
  mutation controls are deliberate online decisions by the organism.
- Local release of carried food/material is a possible later primitive; moving
  vegetation as if it were a rigid object is not an approved implementation.

No world-age assistance, care reward, new signal semantics, reproductive mode,
reservoir gating or additional action was introduced by this handoff.
