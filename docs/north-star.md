# North star

Let blind heredity discover viable life cycles through consistent, paid physical
tradeoffs. Preserve sensing, memory, plasticity, ecology and interaction. Allow
investment before birth, independent gathering, received food and combinations
to compete without rewarding a preferred social strategy.

Balance means plausible opportunities with meaningful costs, not equal outcomes.
Measure the connected chain: food intake, net energy, local encounters, affordable
packets, fusion, juvenile reserves, food-bearing neighbors, actual transfers,
maturation and reproduction. Reservoir turnover is not developmental success.

## Frozen experiment candidate

Version 0.9.1, model `primitive-v44-open-investment`, retains paid packet reserves
after decay and fusion loss. Selected manufacture requires actual energy covering
the full inherited packet size; output 8 controls transfer amount only. Fresh worlds
start with 8,192 mature founders. Fractional gathering uses stochastic rounding.
Other biological rules remain unchanged. No scripted controller enters production.

Controlled foragers demonstrate that high paid investment can support maturation.
Matched short trials support greater encounter opportunity at higher density and
more births when the redundant fraction gate is removed. Unassisted descendant
maturation and reproduction remain unproven. These findings motivate a long
experiment, not a claim that evolution will succeed given enough ticks.

The owner has chosen to stop tuning and run 20–50 million ticks. Life-cycle closure
is a research outcome, not a prerequisite for this candidate. Freeze the biology,
complete engineering checks, commit and push main, and let that experiment decide.
Do not restart the abandoned reservoir B comparison or optimize toward birth counts.

Production admits newborn genomes to the blind hereditary reservoir. A genome can
therefore return as an adult founder without having matured in its birth world.
Separate that search mechanism from genuine descendant-to-descendant reproduction.
