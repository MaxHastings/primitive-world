# North star

Favor continuing life cycles while keeping new mutations in circulation. Preserve
physical costs, sensing, memory, plasticity, ecology and all existing reproductive
and developmental mechanisms. No preferred feeding or social policy is supplied.

## Frozen depth-retention experiment

Version 0.9.2, model `primitive-v45-depth-retention`, changes only hereditary-pool
replacement relative to v44. Every newborn challenges two randomly drawn entries;
the shallower stored ancestry depth loses, with random tie-breaking. The newborn
can replace a deeper record: there is no permanent elite or admission threshold.
Pool evidence persists across saves; physical founder ancestry resets to zero.
See [the exact evolutionary contract](evolution.md).

This is an explicit selection preference for multigenerational reproduction, not
a claim of entirely unassisted ecological selection. Do not add further tuning
during the long experiment. Compare newly achieved within-world reproductive
depth, maturation, persistence and births; stored pool depth alone is not success.

In v44, a copied evolved population produced two directly tracked maturations;
history showed rare depth 2 births, followed by 106 worlds without another. Those
findings motivate this rule; they do not establish that v45 improves outcomes.
The owner has authorized a fresh seed 3001 wallpaper run at 32x after validation
and push to main. The long run determines whether the new rule helps.
