# Completion contract

Status: **active; not achieved**. Set September 4, 2026 after acknowledging
premature completion of the broader project goal. Do not weaken this contract
without explicit user approval.

Deliver a compelling, runnable artificial-life playground with demonstrated
inherited adaptation to changing resources, using minimal coherent mechanisms.

## Required evidence

1. Audit senses, controller state, actions, physical accounting, ecology and
   inheritance against implementation. Disclose authored priors, calibration and
   selection rules. No scripted migration, privileged food destinations, forced
   rescue, population floors or rewards for preferred-looking behavior.
2. Freeze executable, candidate bank, settings, outcome definitions and comparison
   protocol before final evaluation. Eight fresh, previously unused seeds; each
   arm runs 200,000 ticks or to extinction. Prepared and matched unprepared
   founders use identical bodies, initialization procedures, ecology and ordinary
   within-world reproduction/mutation. Prepared descendants must survive in
   **at least six of eight** worlds, and **at least two more** than baseline.
3. In at least six prepared worlds, record depleted-area departure, travel across
   resource-poor space, feeding at a distinct resource patch, then reproduction,
   across at least three separate major resource relocations per world. This is
   lineage persistence, not a requirement for one individual to outlive several
   relocations. Register event definitions before evaluation. Survival, path
   length or endpoint displacement alone cannot establish this.
4. No invalid controller outputs, unexplained population accounting failures or
   hidden rescues. Each final prepared world may spend at most five percent of
   sampled time at or above 95 percent of capacity. Preserve failed runs, exact
   parameters, hashes and all results. Failed validation seeds become development
   data, never reused as supposedly fresh holdouts.
5. Clean launch, clear build/founder identity, usable inspection and persistence,
   defaults matching evaluation, proportionate verification and honest docs.
   Work headlessly, preserve personal files, commit appropriate completed work to
   main. The user's confirmation of the desired play experience is also required;
   automated tests cannot certify fun.

These numerical thresholds are engineering acceptance targets, not statistical
significance or proof of general intelligence. Research uncertainty is not
success. More effort, one good seed, an attractive curve or a tested release does
not complete the goal.
