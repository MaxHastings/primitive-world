**Historical protocol, superseded as a recommendation.** The user chose to return
to random-origin populations after the action-suppression audit. Frozen copies
remain authoritative for existing campaigns; do not alter or restart those runs.
See reports/RANDOM_ORIGIN_DECISION.md. The protocol below records the original

## Authorship boundary

The optional initializer writes ordinary primitive-v3 genomes only. It supplies
collection preference, reserve-conditioned reproduction, substantial offspring
energy investment, local food-difference motor responses and small population-wide
isotropically varied drift. Social action logits start lower. Subsequent audit
proved that transfer, force and emit cannot win for any hidden state in any of
the 256 original genomes; mutation is required before these actions can occur.
Thresholds and gains vary across256 genomes; remaining circuits have small random
connections. No entry is protected against normal birth or offline mutation.

These are real authored behavior priors, including local food steering. Do not
call them learned, bias-free or a mutation-free template. They supply neither
long-distance destinations nor a migration state machine, communication meanings,
cooperation incentives, hidden senses, extra food or different body rules.

## Founding check (register before execution)

Initializer seed9042603;256genomes;1000founders; four already registered cases
with seeds9042701,9042702,9042703,9042704; rotations0,1,2,3;16384tick cap.
Use habitat contrast.25 and regeneration.01; all other body/environment defaults
unchanged, evolving geography enabled. This forgiving environment lowers spatial
contrast, not energy costs. No interventions or rescues. End each case on extinction.

Basic founding gate: at least3/4 worlds reach the cap alive, each qualifying world
records births to descendant parents and maximum depth>=3; no invalid outputs or
population/death-accounting errors. Since founders live at most11000ticks, endpoint
survivors must be descendants. Report population and capacity exposure, even though
this narrow founding gate is not the final capacity/endurance/migration gate.

Run the same four seeds at full contrast as a separately labelled spatial challenge,
without using those challenge endpoints to tune the initializer. Freeze genes,
executable, settings and case list before either suite. This is starter development
testing, not proof of inherited adaptation or statistical significance.

If the starter fails, preserve its results and inspect why before a new explicitly
versioned design. Never silently replace the bank during a registered suite.

## Following a viable result

Keep the starter as a named optional bank, then seed independent training islands
from it. Reuse the existing family-persistence objective, physiological costs and
bounded varied curriculum. Preserve alternatives and recovery contexts; do not
reward named abilities or monotonically increase the death rate.

If the founding gate passes, run a paired100-round campaign from that frozen bank:
root seed9042602,4 islands,64 families,8replicas,200k final development evaluation.
These match the random campaign's budget, training seed schedule and fixed benchmark
and evaluation seeds. Curriculum levels may diverge with competence; compare fixed
benchmarks and final cases, not raw scores on different training levels. The bank
is archived by --initial-bank; there is no additional initialization noise. Normal
offspring mutation and5% random outer-loop immigrants remain enabled.

Freeze and compare prepared pools against THEIR starting bank in separate worlds.
To compare initializer strategies, use equal selection budgets and matched bodies;
the older small random pilot alone is not a fair training-budget comparison. Passing
the founding gate does not pass GOAL_CONTRACT.md. User inspection and the broad
survival/migration gates remain outstanding. No main/default auto-promotion.
